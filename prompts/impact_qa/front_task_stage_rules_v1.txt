TASK
Review an IMPACT front-view event using the visible video, action/step annotations, and the applicable object references below. Annotations are fallible observations, not proof that an error occurred. Judge the target interval; surrounding video may clarify prerequisites or a later correction but must retain its own timestamps.

OBJECT AND STAGE REFERENCES
Model A: the two long adapter-plate screws use the red/yellow Phillips driver and corresponding nuts. Bearing-plate screws and the locking-lever screw use the green/black Torx driver. Torx has a six-lobed tip, not a hex tip. The silver combination wrench operates the internal gear/shaft retaining nut. The official M6 identity conflicts with some M4 action labels; report that conflict rather than inventing a size.
Model B: reviewed bearing-plate disassembly uses the black/blue flat-head driver. Do not copy A's adapter fastening or lever assembly to B. Confirm the actual fastener before prescribing a tool.
Finger starting before tool tightening, and finger removal after tool loosening, can be normal. The side handle uses its own threaded stud. Supporting, inserting, aligning, or checking a shaft can legitimately use the hands. Rotating a shaft is not automatically equivalent to loosening its retaining nut. Distinguish relative nut/shaft motion and the current objective. Colors assist identification; they are not sufficient evidence alone.

RULES
1. Determine model, actual component/fastener, intended local step, prerequisites, actual tool contact, both hands' roles, and visible progress. Mark any hidden item unknown. A coarse step name or generic screw label does not identify the precise fastener. ASR changes assist but do not prove mechanical completion.
2. Handling: an evidenced failure of manipulation, control, or the method required at this stage. Exclude legitimate pre-threading, post-loosening finger removal, support, alignment/testing, and controlled temporary placement.
3. Spatial: an evidenced wrong location/orientation relative to an applicable placement or mating requirement. Reaching toward a bin or briefly resting a tool is insufficient without actual placement and its reference.
4. Temporal: sustained failure to initiate or advance a ready task, or repeated ineffective manipulation, after excluding other-hand progress, necessary alignment/checking, and productive work. Eight seconds is a screening setting, not an official error threshold. Do not infer thinking or hesitation as a mental fact. NULL and hold are not proof of inactivity.
5. Procedural: violation of a supported prerequisite or necessary step. Allow valid alternative orders, trial fitting, and recovery. Dismount during reassembly is a candidate for review, not automatic error.
6. Wrong tool: the actual tool is incompatible with the identified target interface or required stage. Merely holding a tool while fingers work is insufficient.
7. Wrong part: the identified part/fastener is inappropriate for the current target. A correct part with wrong orientation, wrong timing, or poor manipulation must not be relabeled wrong identity without evidence.
8. Categories may coexist only when each has its own evidence. Keep original GT labels separate from independent findings. Insufficient visibility is not Correct. Do not supply a cause simply to justify a GT label.

EXAMPLES
The green driver loosens a screw, fingers then remove it, and the part separates: describe that sequence. Tool co-holding alone does not support Handling or Temporal.
A part is held while the other hand productively loosens its fastener: supporting is not a whole-task stall. A held part with ready prerequisites and a sustained, visibly unproductive interval can be a Temporal candidate; require task-progress evidence.
A component is withdrawn during reassembly and the same component is repositioned and reinserted: consider alignment/recovery before Procedural or Wrong part.

OUTPUT
Return a concise JSON object with target_interval_s, model, target_identity, stage, expected_step_and_source, observed_actions, tool_contact, progress, GT_labels, independent_labels, and checks. For each relevant category, checks must contain verdict (supported, contradicted, or insufficient), timestamped visual evidence, applicable requirement and its source, and the strongest reasonable alternative explanation. Report annotation_conflicts and unresolved_items separately. Cite only supplied frames/timestamps. Do not claim complete installation, internal engagement, torque, exact thread size, or a correction when those facts are not visible or otherwise supported.
