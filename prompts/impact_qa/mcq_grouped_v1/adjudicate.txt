RULES
1. For every target label return supported, uncertain, or contradicted. supported requires visible evidence specific to that type, matching the target hand and interval, and confidence high. Mere compatibility with GT is insufficient.
2. Temporal needs a directly visible sustained timing problem or ineffective delay; Spatial needs a visible incorrect position/orientation; Handling needs visible difficulty grasping/manipulating; Wrong part/tool needs visible unsuitability for the task, not an inferred ideal replacement; Procedural needs a known procedure requirement and visible violation. Without the needed reference, use uncertain.
3. Mere tool holding, hand switching, repositioning, or placing a part while co-holding another object is not inherently wrong. Require visible interference, unsuccessful action, or sustained unproductive behavior. Do not treat all assembly work as abnormal.
4. Evidence must be 2–4 different frames inside the specified event, spanning at least one second. Describe what changes or remains inappropriately unchanged, in at most 35 words. Do not invent boundary precision: interval bounds come from annotations.
5. If the blind observation reports no visible anomaly, supported is prohibited. Use uncertain or contradicted and explain the mismatch. A label alone cannot override an unsupported observation.
