For every selected open-QA topic, generate BOTH a natural question and its answer from the video and supplied GT facts. The input contains a topic and evidence, not a prewritten question or answer. Return exactly one QA for each selected candidate ID, with source fact IDs and evidence frame IDs.

Rules:
- Write a clear question a viewer could answer about this clip. Do not copy the topic sentence verbatim.
- Ground answer claims in the supplied GT facts. Use video frames to check that the topic fits the clip. Do not invent tool-to-part contact, completion, causes, corrections, or actions.
- Keep question types separate: overall asks what the clip does; detailed asks for main operation phases; completion asks about one component; order compares two distinct operations; duration asks elapsed time or clip-relative bounds.
- Preserve exact names when mentioning a specific component, tool, or location-specific variant. Do not combine different named parts.
- Never mention GT, annotations, labels, IDs, model selection, or the dataset in public Q/A text.
- Cite only supplied source fact IDs. Evidence frames must be among the selected frames and support the answer. If the answer is supported by GT but not independently visible, say so in evidence_note and use cannot_independently_confirm.

Answer style:
- Overall: one or two sentences, no more than 55 words. State assembly/disassembly, mention at least one supplied tool and one to three main objects. Summarize; do not list every screw, washer, small motion, or GT item.
- Detailed: at most four sentences and 95 words. Describe the main tool/component operation phases in chronological order. Do not repeat hand changes, holds, transfers, or every atomic action. Name a tool-part relation only when visible.
- Completion: follow the supplied yes/no polarity. For a negative answer, begin with “No”, state that the queried component was not completed, then name what component operation did complete in this clip. Do not make every answer “Yes”.
- Order: ask which of two distinct component operations happened first. Answer with the first operation before the second. Report observed order; do not judge whether it was required or correct.
- Duration: ask about the supplied single-target operation episode. Give the exact supplied duration or exact clip-relative start/end times. Do not call it complete unless GT explicitly says it completed. Do not use source-video timestamps or round the supplied values.
