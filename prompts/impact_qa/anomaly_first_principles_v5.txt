PURPOSE
Inspect an IMPACT task interval using video, task constraints, and fallible annotations. These are provisional project rules, not an official annotation manual. Report observable facts first, then assess errors. Preserve raw labels separately.

RULES
1. Identify the product variant, hand(s), actual tool, component, interface, starting state, intended outcome, and observed result. Tool color aids identification but does not establish compatibility. A and B may require different tools and assembly relations.
2. Wrong tool: the tool actually applied is incompatible with the identified interface or operation. A same-target tool change is supporting evidence, not proof by itself.
3. Wrong part: the selected component or fastener identity/specification is unsuitable for the required role. Reorienting or retrying the same correct component does not establish wrong identity. Record uncertain wrong-target interpretations separately.
4. Spatial: the observed destination, position, or orientation violates an identified task-specific constraint. Verify placement/release and component state. Temporary placement and moving an obstruction are not automatically errors.
5. Handling: observable loss of control or failed manipulation, such as an unintended dropped part or unsupported release. Distinguish deliberate placement, coordinated two-hand work, inspection, and legitimate adjustment. Do not invent force, damage, slipping, or risk.
6. Procedural: identify the required prerequisite, show it was not satisfied, and show the attempted transition. Independent steps may be reordered. Reopening and reworking alone do not prove a violation; a cover may be temporarily tried for fit.
7. Temporal: cite an explicit timing constraint, or provide evidence of avoidable lack of progress while ruling out both-hand work, inspection, necessary retries, and waiting. Duration, repetition, and the production 5-second sampling threshold do not define an anomaly.
8. Holding an unused screwdriver or holding it while storing a part does not by itself prove any error. Assess contact, interference, control, and actual progress.
9. An annotation, majority across cameras, later recovery label, or model confidence is not sufficient proof. A same-name temporal match may refer to a different physical attempt. Preserve each camera's timestamps; do not treat candidate matches as synchronized frames.
10. Multiple error types may coexist if each has evidence. No observed violation is not equivalent to Correct. Use unresolved internally when identity, visibility, task constraint, or event correspondence is missing. Never invent an answer to fill a class quota.

OUTPUT
Return concise facts with source frame IDs/times; the applicable constraint and its source; raw_annotation; independent_assessment (supported_candidate / suspected_annotation_issue / insufficient_evidence / verified_correct_in_scope); independently supported types; alternative explanations; visibility/matching limits; and QA eligibility. Confidence describes uncertainty and is not a calibrated probability. Keep target evidence separate from context. Preserve reliable factual QA even if the normative cause is unresolved. A cause/type MCQ requires an audited target and supported rule; Correct requires positive verification within the question's scope.

EXAMPLES
Video: right hand holds Torx while left hand removes a loosened part. Assessment: coordinated work is visible; idle-tool Holding does not establish Handling. Do not invent a cause from the GT label.
Video: a small piece leaves the manipulating fingers, moves on the table, and is immediately retrieved. Assessment: local control-loss candidate; distinguish accidental release from allowed placement, and do not infer its exact thread size or damage.
Video: the same black adapter ring is withdrawn, turned, and inserted again. Assessment: orientation/fit facts are supported; wrong component identity is not established.
Video: cover placed, reopened, internal fastening performed, then cover replaced. Assessment: the observed sequence supports a factual question. Premature closure remains a candidate until the necessary prerequisite is verified.
Video: one hand is marked hold for 88 seconds while the other repeatedly works inside the housing. Assessment: do not call the entire interval idle or anomalous solely because it is long.
