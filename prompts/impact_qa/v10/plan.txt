Select evidence-supported question types for an industrial assembly/disassembly video, following the breadth of EgoErrorVQA's Assembly101 subset. You receive a reference-writing packet with the complete task procedure, a prerequisite graph, current action, source annotations, component states, actual event timeline, visible details, and an evidence catalog. No field should be presumed present or trustworthy solely because its name exists. Do not generate answers yet.

Select up to TWO complementary question intents. For attaching/inserting/installing actions, prioritize install_relation or completion when supported; do not replace these with generic hand-motion questions by default. For step boundaries with history, consider observed_order or order_compliance. For error/correction events, consider error_explanation or correction. Normal actions are eligible for completion and installation checks. No fixed yes/no balance or mandatory error premise.

Types and minimum support:
- action_correctness: an applicable source action with explicit phase/error label, or a supported comparison with an authoritative requirement. Limit correctness to that action.
- install_relation: actor/component/receiver identities and attachment relation, plus a corresponding result state or explicit successful/failed completion annotation. An action name alone cannot establish a resulting connection. A correct component state supports its specified assembly relation only if a verified mapping connects that component to the receiver in this device variant.
- completion: an explicit completion event, time-specific component state, or sufficiently clear final-state evidence. A normal hand action is not proof that the entire coarse step is complete. A state already correct before this interval does not prove installation happened during this interval; select an end-state question instead.
- observed_order: two timed events within the question's evidence window. This asks what occurred before/after, without asserting a requirement.
- reference_order: two timed actions or component states plus an explicitly supplied illustrated reference sequence. Ask whether the observed order follows the order shown in that guide, not whether it is the only correct order. Say 'shown in the guide' in the question. A component state does not establish completion of all components in its coarse subassembly.
- order_compliance: an applicable authoritative prerequisite rule plus adequate observations/states to establish whether it held when the target action began; alternatively an explicit source order-error annotation may support a coarse order-error answer but cannot identify an unspecified missing predecessor. A mined edge alone is not authoritative.
- omission: a required step and covered history up to a justified deadline, including initial state and any invalidation/removal. Not seeing a step in a short clip does not establish omission.
- unnecessary_action: explicit source annotation or an applicable optional/required-step rule plus execution evidence. Repetition can be a legal retry.
- tool_part_location: sourced identity/relation, and a specification if the question asks what should have been used or where it should have gone.
- correction: source recovery label with a supported action; recovery success additionally needs a result state. An ASR -1 to 1 change alone does not identify a PPR recovery event.
- error_explanation: explicit error description or an observed violation of an authoritative requirement. Use only documented reasons.

Separate task variants, assembly/disassembly, component instances, and attempts. Prerequisite edges mean constraints, not one unique linear ordering. AND prerequisites all apply; OR alternatives require their condition. Unconnected nodes are not automatically interchangeable if the graph is incomplete. Past completion may be invalidated by removal. Simultaneous bimanual actions do not imply a strict order.

Evidence from another camera must have verified synchronization and object mapping before it can establish a claim about an ego interval. A single uniform 16-frame clip is not assumed to cover full history or fine completion details. List missing evidence and propose the smallest relevant window/close-up expansion rather than inventing it.
