# IMPACT A/B 安装与拆卸：中英文参考文本

版本：2026-09-29。适用范围：IMPACT实验中拆装的角磨机组件，不是整机维修工艺。A参考官方手册；B参考官方结构图及局部视频证据。下面是一种参考组织顺序，独立步骤可合法换序。未核实的槽口规格、扭矩、螺纹方向、小件叠放和保持方式不补写为事实。

## 中文版

### A型：Fein CG15-125BL

**安装**

1. **安装转子轴组件。** 扶稳黑色齿轮箱壳体，将转子轴组件对准壳体的对应通道，使轴端进入齿轮腔。转子轴组件与轴承板上的输出轴是不同部件。
2. **安装小锥齿轮和内部保持螺母。** 将小锥齿轮在转子轴端就位，先用手指把保持螺母旋到位，再用银色两用扳手紧固。这里不强行指定M4/M6，因为部分动作标注与官方组件名称存在冲突。
3. **安装适配板。** 将适配板在转子进入壳体的一侧对位。两颗长螺丝分别与对应M4螺母配合，用红黄十字改锥操作螺丝端，将适配板连接到壳体。核对两组连接，不以完成一颗螺丝代表整块板已固定。
4. **安装轴承板组件。** 对齐轴承板与壳体开口，使输出轴朝外、背面大齿轮朝向齿轮腔。板组件就位后，用绿黑Torx六瓣梅花改锥操作两颗轴承板固定螺丝。外观贴合不能证明内部已经正确啮合。
5. **安装锁紧拨杆组件。** 核对拨杆、弹簧、垫圈和拨杆螺丝，在对应位置装配并用绿黑Torx改锥操作拨杆螺丝。弹簧与垫圈的精确叠放尚未确认，不能仅据这份文字确定内部装配顺序。
6. **安装侧手柄并检查。** 将侧手柄自身的螺纹柱对准壳体侧孔，徒手旋入。检查适配板两组连接、轴承板两颗螺丝、拨杆组件和侧手柄的可见状态；这不替代扭矩或功能检验。

**拆卸**

1. **拆下侧手柄。** 扶稳壳体，徒手旋出侧手柄自身的螺纹柱，取下并归置手柄。
2. **拆下拨杆组件。** 用绿黑Torx改锥松开拨杆螺丝，控制并收纳螺丝、拨杆、弹簧与垫圈，记录实际叠放关系，避免小件散落。
3. **拆下轴承板组件。** 用绿黑Torx改锥解除两颗轴承板螺丝的紧固；螺丝松动后可以用手旋出。两处连接解除后，支撑并取下轴承板组件，露出齿轮腔。
4. **解除适配板连接。** 用红黄十字改锥松开两颗长螺丝，控制并收纳配套M4螺母。螺丝已解除不一定表示适配板能立即取下，应结合轴组件的实际阻挡关系判断。
5. **分离内部传动件与壳体。** 用银色两用扳手松开内部保持螺母，取下螺母和小锥齿轮；确认相关约束已解除后，分离转子轴组件、壳体和适配板，并整理各部件与紧固件。

### B型：Fein WSG7-115A

**安装——按结构关系整理的参考流程**

1. **对位转子轴组件与壳体。** 将转子轴端对准银灰色齿轮箱壳体的通道，使图示支承法兰与壳体连接面对应。铜色换向器位于远离齿轮腔的一端。
2. **安装小锥齿轮和保持螺母。** 按官方图示，使小锥齿轮及保持螺母在轴端就位。六角螺母接口对应匹配扳手；B的具体扳手规格和轴止转方法尚未核验，这一工具关系属于接口推定。
3. **固定壳体与轴组件的连接。** 操作两颗壳体连接螺丝，使壳体与图示轴组件支承法兰连接。已检查B拆卸样例在该区域使用红黄十字改锥；装回时仍需匹配实际槽口。这两颗螺丝不是A型适配板的长螺丝与M4螺母配对结构。
4. **安装远端适配板。** 将深色适配板对位到官方图所示的转子远端，核对其方向和配合位置。确切卡扣、压装或其他保持方式未确认，不据A型结构补加螺丝或螺母。
5. **安装轴承板组件。** 将银色轴承板与壳体开口对齐，输出轴朝外，再操作两颗固定螺丝。工具须匹配实际槽口；已核对的B拆卸样例使用黑蓝一字改锥，但不能据此要求所有B型螺丝都使用一字工具。
6. **安装侧手柄并检查。** 徒手将侧手柄自身螺纹柱旋入壳体侧孔，检查壳体连接螺丝、轴承板连接、远端适配板和手柄的可见状态。官方B图未单列A型的拨杆、弹簧、垫圈小组件，不应直接增加相同安装步骤。

**拆卸——按连接解除关系整理的参考流程**

1. **拆下侧手柄。** 扶稳壳体，徒手旋出并取下侧手柄。
2. **拆下轴承板组件。** 按实际槽口选择工具，解除两颗固定螺丝后取下银色轴承板。已检查B样例在这一操作中使用黑蓝一字改锥。
3. **分离远端适配板。** 按其真实连接方式解除保持并取下，不因动画表现为轴向移动就认为可以直接强拉。该件与其他独立操作的相对次序可以不同。
4. **解除壳体连接螺丝。** 松开两颗连接壳体与轴组件支承法兰的螺丝。已检查样例使用红黄十字改锥；按实际螺丝区分长度和位置，不套用A型M4螺母关系。
5. **解除内部保持连接并分离轴组件。** 在能接近齿轮腔后，用与接口匹配的工具解除保持螺母，取下小锥齿轮；相关连接全部解除后，再分离转子轴组件与壳体，并归置零件。

共同的关键约束是：先对位、后紧固；先解除有关连接、后分离部件。侧手柄、适配板等独立操作的合法换序，不能仅凭与示范顺序不同就判为异常。

## English version

Scope: These descriptions cover the components manipulated in the IMPACT experiments, not complete power-tool servicing. Model A follows the available official manual. Model B is a reference sequence based on the official component diagram and limited video evidence, not a fully verified manufacturing procedure. Independent operations may occur in a different valid order.

### Model A: Fein CG15-125BL

**Assembly**

1. **Insert the rotor/drive-shaft assembly.** Support the black gearbox housing and align the shaft assembly with the corresponding passage, bringing the shaft end into the gear cavity. This assembly is distinct from the output spindle on the bearing plate.
2. **Fit the small bevel gear and retaining nut.** Seat the small bevel gear on the shaft end. Start the retaining nut by hand, then tighten it with the silver combination wrench. Its size is not fixed as M4 or M6 here because some action labels conflict with the official component naming.
3. **Attach the adapter plate.** Align the plate on the side where the rotor enters the housing. Pair each of the two long screws with its corresponding M4 nut, and operate the screw heads with the red/yellow Phillips screwdriver. Check both connections; completing one screw does not establish that the entire plate is secured.
4. **Install the bearing-plate assembly.** Align it with the housing opening, with the output spindle facing outward and the larger gear on the back facing the gear cavity. Once seated, operate its two retaining screws with the green/black Torx screwdriver. A flush appearance alone does not establish correct internal gear engagement.
5. **Install the locking-lever assembly.** Account for the lever, spring, washer and retaining screw. Assemble them at the intended location and operate the retaining screw with the green/black Torx screwdriver. The exact spring/washer stack remains unverified and must not be inferred from this text.
6. **Attach the side handle and inspect.** Thread the handle's own mounting stud into the side socket by hand. Inspect both adapter connections, both bearing-plate screws, the lever assembly and the handle. This visual check does not replace torque or functional verification.

**Disassembly**

1. **Remove the side handle.** Support the housing, unscrew the handle's mounting stud by hand and put the handle aside.
2. **Remove the lever assembly.** Loosen its retaining screw with the green/black Torx screwdriver. Control and retain the screw, lever, spring and washer, recording their actual arrangement and preventing loss of small parts.
3. **Remove the bearing-plate assembly.** Release both retaining screws with the green/black Torx screwdriver. Once loose, the screws may be turned out by hand. Support and remove the plate assembly after both connections have been released, exposing the gear cavity.
4. **Release the adapter connections.** Use the red/yellow Phillips screwdriver to loosen the two long screws and retain their paired M4 nuts. Releasing these screws does not necessarily allow immediate removal of the adapter; check for remaining constraints from the shaft assembly.
5. **Separate the internal transmission parts and housing.** Loosen the retaining nut with the silver combination wrench, then remove the nut and small bevel gear. Once the relevant constraints have been released, separate the rotor/shaft assembly, housing and adapter plate, and organize the parts and fasteners.

### Model B: Fein WSG7-115A

**Assembly — a reference sequence organized by component connections**

1. **Align the rotor/shaft assembly with the housing.** Bring the shaft end into the passage of the silver-gray gearbox housing and align the illustrated support flange with the housing interface. The copper-colored commutator remains at the end away from the gear cavity.
2. **Fit the small bevel gear and retaining nut.** Position these components on the shaft end as shown in the official diagram. A matching wrench is appropriate to the illustrated hexagonal nut interface; the exact B-specific wrench size and shaft-restraint method remain unverified. This tool relationship is an interface-based inference.
3. **Secure the housing-to-shaft-support connections.** Operate the two housing screws connecting the housing to the illustrated shaft-assembly support flange. An inspected B removal example uses the red/yellow Phillips screwdriver in this region; installation still requires a bit matching the actual recess. These are not the A-model adapter screw/M4-nut pairs.
4. **Position the far-end adapter plate.** Align the dark adapter plate with the far end of the rotor shown in the official diagram. Check its orientation and mating position. Its exact clip, press-fit or other retention mechanism remains unverified; do not add A-style screws or nuts by analogy.
5. **Install the bearing-plate assembly.** Align the silver plate with the housing opening, with its output spindle facing outward, and secure the two retaining screws. Match the tool to the actual screw recess. An inspected B disassembly example uses the black/blue flat-head screwdriver; this does not establish it as the required tool for every B-model screw.
6. **Attach the side handle and inspect.** Thread the handle's own stud into the side socket by hand. Inspect the housing screws, bearing-plate connections, far-end adapter and handle. The official B diagram does not separately show the A-model lever/spring/washer assembly, so those A-specific installation steps should not be added automatically.

**Disassembly — a reference sequence organized by releasing connections**

1. **Remove the side handle.** Support the housing and unscrew the handle by hand.
2. **Remove the bearing-plate assembly.** Use a tool matching the actual recesses to release both retaining screws, then remove the silver plate. The inspected B example uses the black/blue flat-head screwdriver for this operation.
3. **Separate the far-end adapter plate.** Release its actual retention mechanism before removal. An axial movement in the animation does not establish that the real part can simply be pulled off. Its order relative to other independent operations may vary.
4. **Release the housing connection screws.** Loosen the two screws joining the housing to the shaft-assembly support flange. The inspected example uses the red/yellow Phillips screwdriver. Preserve their actual length/position associations rather than applying the A-model M4-nut arrangement.
5. **Release the internal retention and separate the shaft assembly.** Once the gear cavity is accessible, release the retaining nut with a matching tool and remove the small bevel gear. Separate the rotor/shaft assembly from the housing only after all relevant connections have been released, then organize the parts.

The shared constraints are to align before fastening and release the relevant connections before separating parts. A different valid order of independent operations, such as handling the side handle or adapter plate, is not by itself a procedural anomaly.
